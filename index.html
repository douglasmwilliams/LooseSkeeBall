Model and limitations

Predicted score centers are the previously agreed adjusted lane estimates, with Week 1 contributing to team strength but not lane effects. Estimates are partially pooled toward the league averages; the 1,143 Lane 2 game remains included for FUCK. Score variability is estimated from complete recorded games and stabilized toward the league-wide spread. Win probabilities assume approximately normal single-match scores and independence between teams. Historical markers use recorded Weeks 2–8 scores on the chosen lane, excluding 270-point forfeits/defaults. Win percentages are estimates, not measured head-to-head frequencies. Games can have strategic hundreds effects not fully represented by normal curves.

80% ranges describe possible match scores, not confidence intervals for the true team mean. Sample counts are provided to show how much direct lane evidence exists.
